## Reviewer checklist

For every PRIVATE-FIGURE FLAG above:

1. Is the person identifiable by role alone (e.g. "the procurement officer")? If yes, rewrite to drop the name.
2. Is the person a public-facing official (regular media appearances, signs official communications)? If yes, document why they qualify and leave in.
3. Is the conduct described as alleged in the body prose? Spot-check; the rewrite pass should have caught this but verify.
4. Does the case page show the AllegationBanner above the h1? Yes if evidentiary_status is "alleged" or "reported" (now enforced in the template).

For every alleged-tier case overall:

1. Body lede explicitly says "alleged" before any conduct verb.
2. Headline does not assert conduct as fact (no "Senator X used public resources" — must be "alleged use of public resources").
3. Sources include the underlying complaint or docket filing, not just news coverage.
4. Legal status field accurately reflects the docket (complaint_filed, closed_no_action, etc.).
