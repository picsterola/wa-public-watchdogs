## Why it's in the registry

This is a Tier 1 documented LEB proceeding involving an elected state Representative and a conflict-of-interest allegation. ## Reform implication

The LEB docket in 2025 includes at least five conflict-of-interest proceedings against House members. This volume indicates a systemic rather than isolated problem. Proactive, public conflict-of-interest disclosures — combined with a cross-referencing mechanism against committee assignments and votes — would reduce both the incidence of conflicts and the reactive burden on the LEB.
