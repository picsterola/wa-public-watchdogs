## What happened

The Washington State Legislative Ethics Board issued Opinion 25-03 on April 10, 2025, concerning a complaint against the Washington State House Democratic Caucus. The subject matter is identified in the LEB docket as "Blocking User from Official Caucus Social Media." The LEB issued a formal opinion on this date, indicating the complaint was reviewed and adjudicated.

## Why it's in the registry

This is a Tier 1 documented LEB proceeding involving an official legislative caucus and the use of official social media accounts to restrict public access. The conduct category — blocking constituents from official government-operated social media — has been the subject of significant legal scrutiny nationally. The registry includes this case as part of the documented 2024-2026 pattern of LEB proceedings.

## Reform implication

Official legislative social media accounts maintained with public resources are subject to First Amendment constraints that campaign accounts are not. Clear guidance distinguishing official from campaign accounts — and prohibiting viewpoint-based blocking on official accounts — would resolve the ambiguity that generates complaints of this type.
