## Why it's in the registry

This is a Tier 1 documented LEB proceeding involving an elected state Representative and an outside-employment conflict-of-interest allegation. It is one of three outside-employment conflicts reaching the LEB in 2025 (alongside 25-04 and 25-06), reflecting a structural gap in the legislature's conflict management framework.

## Reform implication

Three outside-employment conflict-of-interest complaints reaching formal LEB proceedings in a single year indicates the current disclosure regime is insufficient to identify and manage these conflicts proactively. A mandatory annual outside-income disclosure, cross-referenced against committee assignments and legislative votes, would reduce both the incidence of conflicts and the burden on the LEB complaint process.
